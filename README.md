### Glad you stumbled onto my page! I'm Sanjay Pradeep 👋

*Like the particles I study, you probably got here by a random walk.*

**About me:** I'm a Ph.D. candidate in [Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering](https://www.mae.ucla.edu/) at [UCLA](https://www.ucla.edu), advised by [Prof. Jeff Eldredge](https://samueli.ucla.edu/people/jeff-eldredge/), and I also work with the [UCLA Fielding School of Public Health](https://ph.ucla.edu). I hold an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from UCLA and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from [IIT Palakkad](https://iitpkd.ac.in), and I have previously interned with [Dassault Systèmes SIMULIA](https://www.3ds.com/products/simulia). I'm originally from Trivandrum, a beautiful coastal city near the southern tip of India.

**Research:** My current research lies at the intersection of computational fluid dynamics (CFD), artificial intelligence (AI), and public health. My core focus is building neural surrogates for the hydrodynamics of particles in Stokes flow. I also apply AI to electron microscopy, and CFD to aerosol deposition in exposure studies and air-quality samplers.

**Some of my work:**

- **Neural surrogates for Stokes flow:** equivariant networks that predict how irregularly shaped particles move through viscous fluids. [[arXiv]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23105)
- **AI for electron microscopy:** using vision foundation models to find, segment, and classify particles in electron micrographs. Under review at *Scientific Reports*. [[arXiv]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06673) · [SAM-SEM-Segmentation](https://github.com/SanjayPradeep97/SAM-SEM-Segmentation)
- **CFD of aerosol deposition:** simulating where aerosol particles end up inside air-quality samplers, which leads to better estimates of people's exposure to harmful airborne particles.

**What I'm working on now:** I'm building neural surrogates that speed up particle simulations by orders of magnitude:

- Equivariant neural networks that predict how randomly shaped particles interact with each other
- Scaling these up to simulations of millions of unique, randomly shaped particles
- Extending them to microswimmers (active Stokesian dynamics) and flexible fibers

Much of this work is being published or prepared for arXiv, and I'll make the code repos public as each paper comes out. I've also built some large datasets that will go public too, so check back soon!
